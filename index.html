💖 My Dearest Love 💖

Aaj ka din mere liye sirf tumhara birthday nahi hai 🎂, balkay wo din hai jab meri zindagi ko uski sab se khoobsurat wajah mili 💕✨

Tum sirf mera pyaar nahi ho ❤️, tum meri aadat ho 💫, meri zaroorat ho 🤍, meri har khushi ki wajah ho 😊💖

Door ho kar bhi tum hamesha mere paas rehte ho 🫶, har khayal mein 💭, har dua mein 🤲, har muskurahat ke peeche tum hi ho ✨💗

Kabhi kabhi itna miss karti hoon 😔💞 ke bas dil karta hai waqt ko rok loon ⏳ aur seedha tumhare paas aa jaoon 🤍🥺

Humara long distance mushkil zaroor hai 🌍💔, lekin mera pyaar tumhare liye har distance se zyada strong hai 💪💖💫

Aur InshaAllah next year hum sirf call pe nahi 📱, balkay ek saath 🤝, ek hi jagah 🏡, ek hi cake cut karenge 🎂🎉💖

Tum meri life ka wo hissa ho jisko main kabhi khona nahi chahti 💕🥺… thoda sa pagal 🤭, thoda sa shararti 😏, lekin sirf mera 😌💘

💌 Happy Birthday meri jaan 🎉💖 I love you more than anything ❤️‍🔥🤍
